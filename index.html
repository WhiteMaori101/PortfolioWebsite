About

Joshua is a passionate and aspiring actor. He first worked on film as a child and went on to play leading roles in amateur musical theatre.

He trained at South Seas Film and Television School and has since appeared in a run of short films alongside his stage work. He brings a wide accent range, a physical skill set and musicianship, including taonga pūoro, to every role.
